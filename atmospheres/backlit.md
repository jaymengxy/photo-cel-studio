# backlit

## When to use
Clearly observed rear sun/window/stage light or silhouetted subject rim; source-derived or user-requested.

## Source evidence
Subject dark-side form with bright background and edge illumination; physically grounded light direction.

## Lighting strategy
Use restrained cel rim-light color on appropriate contour segments and readable inner base/shadow shapes.

## Palette
Warm or cool rim based on observed source, neutral dark midtones inside silhouette.

## Rendering grammar
Crisp rim plates, selective contour darkening and consistent face/hair edge separation.

## Compatibility
Compatible with golden-hour when low sun is behind; misty if atmospheric conditions visible.

## Preservation guardrails
Preserve body, facial recognition when possible, pose and direction of actual illumination.

## Negative constraints
No all-over glowing outline, shifted light, black featureless faces unless present in source.

## Quality checks
Backlight feels optically plausible while key identity still survives.
