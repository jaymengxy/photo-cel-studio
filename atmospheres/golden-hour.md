# golden-hour

## When to use
Low-angle warm sun already visible in source or explicitly requested by user.

## Source evidence
Warm direct illumination, long soft-edged geometric cast shadows, setting or rising sun cues.

## Lighting strategy
Expand existing orange/amber highlights to confident graphic shapes; treat shadows as cool blue-violet/cyan complementary cel masses.

## Palette
Warm amber/orange highlights, authentic environment colors and cooler shadows.

## Rendering grammar
Bright low sunlight planes, simplified long cast shadows, source-consistent direction.

## Compatibility
Can combine with backlit when low sun truly falls behind the subject; avoid neon-night automatically.

## Preservation guardrails
Keep time-related shadow direction, silhouette and scene context.

## Negative constraints
No gratuitous new sunset, lens-flare explosion, shifted sun or artificial night.

## Quality checks
Light warmth reads like drawn animation but the source's sun geometry survives.
