# misty

## When to use
Visibly low-contrast distance, real fog, sea mist, valley haze or soft atmospheric occlusion.

## Source evidence
Depth layers diminishing in contrast, edges disappearing with distance, defined near-field objects.

## Lighting strategy
Separate foreground ink/flat colors from reduced-line distant silhouettes; treat mist as distance-dependent, not uniform blur.

## Palette
Muted scene-derived cool/warm colors; less saturated distant layers.

## Rendering grammar
Foreground flat hard-edge cel shadows; distant transitions may be soft where true atmospheric scattering occurs.

## Compatibility
Compatible with landscape-cinematic, architecture-graphic and other scenes when source shows actual mist.

## Preservation guardrails
Keep mountain or building outlines where visible, preserve subject count and distance.

## Negative constraints
No blur over whole picture, fabricated fog, erased subject or surreal smoky spirals.

## Quality checks
Viewer perceives depth and accurate source silhouettes with a sharp hero.
