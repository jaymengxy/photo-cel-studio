# neon-night

## When to use
Street or vehicle or portraits with actually visible night lighting, illuminated signage or artificial colored lamps.

## Source evidence
Dark sky/night exposure, localized red/blue/green light pools, signs, cast colored light.

## Lighting strategy
Organize lamps, signs and reflected light as a few crisp cel-lit zones. Keep dark regions legible.

## Palette
Deep blue-violet/charcoal base, locally saturated observed light colors and neutral skin where appropriate.

## Rendering grammar
Hard-edge colored pools and specular reflections along genuine wet/metal/glass surfaces; no generalized glow haze.

## Compatibility
Compatible with rainy if wet surfaces are genuinely visible; avoid auto pairing with golden-hour as if both described one time.

## Preservation guardrails
Retain the actual location and original artificial light positions.

## Negative constraints
No invented neon signs, illegible writing, new cyberpunk towers or rain not in the photo.

## Quality checks
Night reading, lamp/source continuity and flat cel shadows all remain credible.
