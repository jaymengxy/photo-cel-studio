# rainy

## When to use
Raindrops, umbrellas, glossy wet pavement, rain streaks or water reflections seen in source; explicit requested weather redesign also valid.

## Source evidence
Existing wet roads/windows/garments and their local reflections, visible wet sheen or droplets.

## Lighting strategy
Simplify raindrops into selective purposeful lines and wet regions into a small number of graphic reflection bands.

## Palette
Cool or neutral rainy scene tones, source-grounded signage or clothing accent.

## Rendering grammar
Hard-edged reflected lights and contact shadows, not diffuse full-image gloss.

## Compatibility
Combine with neon-night if source is wet neon street; not automatic from blue clouds alone.

## Preservation guardrails
Keep real pedestrian count, clothing dryness/wetness where visible, streets, actual reflective objects.

## Negative constraints
No new storm, umbrellas, puddles or soaked clothing when merely overcast.

## Quality checks
Weather comes from evidence and does not distort objects to add reflections.
