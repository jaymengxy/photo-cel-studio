# snowy

## When to use
Existing snow, ice, snow-coated roofs, snow-covered streets or branches; explicit requested season conversion.

## Source evidence
Distinct white snow masses, covered edges, tracked texture, cool shadow depth.

## Lighting strategy
Draw clustered snow shapes resting naturally on horizontal surfaces; simplify busy crystals.

## Palette
Snow white, cold blue-gray shadow and source-derived clothing/building accents.

## Rendering grammar
Planar cool shadows and restrained warm reflected light only from real sources.

## Compatibility
May coexist with backlit if visible; do not auto pair with rainy as simultaneous conditions.

## Preservation guardrails
Preserve building/landform contours, snow locations and people or vehicle contact points.

## Negative constraints
No invented blizzard, snowflakes, mountains or relocated drifts.

## Quality checks
Snow coverage and shadows follow real geometry and cel shading remains flat.
