# architecture-graphic

## When to use
Building exteriors, façades, stairs, passageways, stations, bridges and structural close-ups.

## Source cues
repeating windows, railings, stair geometry, verticals, perspectives, façade rhythm.

## Composition strategy
Preserve camera perspective, structure rhythm and key architectural ratios; abstract only low-value material texture.

## Linework
Precise line economy, meaningful high-contrast edges, reduced distant structure detail.

## Palette
Building materials mapped to convincing restrained flat planes; small observed color accents.

## Shadow grammar
Hard-edged cast-shadow planes aligned to visible sun/window light.

## Background policy
Keep actual openings, floors, load-bearing connections, doors and number of steps where recognizable.

## Preservation guardrails
Structural continuity, vanishing lines, geometry, unique landmark details and spatial sense.

## Negative constraints
No extra windows, disappearing stairs, inconsistent perspective, fantasy towers or changed building use.

## Quality checks
Architecture remains spatially and physically credible.
