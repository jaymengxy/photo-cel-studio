# dynamic-action

## When to use
Decisive motion, sprinting, dancing, play, sport, sudden bodily gestures or objects visibly in motion.

## Source cues
body lean, reaching arms, motion blur, direction of travel, strong existing diagonal.

## Composition strategy
Emphasize the original direction and gesture within the given frame; redraw overlap as a readable key-frame silhouette.

## Linework
Confident directional outlines, sparse internal motion contours, clear contact/hand shape.

## Palette
Higher contrast value groups with purposeful accent hues from actual clothing/objects.

## Shadow grammar
Hard directional shadows that reinforce gesture and silhouette.

## Background policy
Simplify secondary background to improve movement clarity; retain ground contact and essential spectators.

## Preservation guardrails
Number of actors, action phase, direction, actual interactions, face/expression and body proportions.

## Negative constraints
Do not add fighting, jumps, swords, speed lines, extra limbs or exaggerated impossible poses by default.

## Quality checks
Viewer sees the same captured action, clearly drawn; no temporal or anatomical drift.
