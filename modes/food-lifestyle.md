# food-lifestyle

## When to use
Meals, desserts, coffee, restaurant tables, cookware and drink photography.

## Source cues
edible shapes, plate/cup silhouette, ingredient colors, sauce patterns, table arrangement.

## Composition strategy
Keep authentic plating and camera angle; use the table plane and crockery as clear graphic supports.

## Linework
Clean serving-object contours; reduce photographic microtexture while retaining meaningful food structure.

## Palette
Preserve ingredient identity hues, warm/cool accents of lighting, simplified appetizing value masses.

## Shadow grammar
Hard-edged dish volume and contact shadows, selective bright gloss as small simple cel highlights.

## Background policy
Retain utensils, tableware and interpersonal eating context; reduce distracting clutter.

## Preservation guardrails
Original food type, visible ingredients, dish counts, container form, arrangement, hands if present.

## Negative constraints
No fake menu text, invented garnish, extra plates, fantasy dishes or unnaturally shiny digital food.

## Quality checks
Dish stays identifiable with cel-drawn materials and genuine plating.
