# interior-atmosphere

## When to use
Rooms, houses, indoor cafés, studios, stations and other designed or lived-in interior photography.

## Source cues
windows, furniture alignment, doorways, floor/wall planes, shelves, ceiling light.

## Composition strategy
Preserve room viewpoint and floor plan relationships, simplify into foreground/mid/background animated stage.

## Linework
Reliable architecture/room edges with softer small-object interior marks; maintain perspective.

## Palette
Original furnishing materials and observed interior lighting condensed into flat complementary hue groups.

## Shadow grammar
Clear planes cast by windows or fixtures; all sources physically consistent.

## Background policy
Recompose noise but not the actual space layout; keep distinctive furnishings and openings.

## Preservation guardrails
Relative placement/quantity of furniture, windows, doors, people, room geometry and object interactions.

## Negative constraints
No invented windows, moving furniture, added décor, misleading extra rooms or fake light sources.

## Quality checks
Room arrangement and perspective survive; cel environment looks designed rather than blurred.
