# landscape-cinematic

## When to use
Mountains, ocean, forest, lake, geology, open sky and natural travel scenery where environment is primary.

## Source cues
distinctive ridge lines, horizon, layered hills, sky masses, vegetation rhythms, water edge.

## Composition strategy
Preserve source viewpoint, unique terrain silhouette and strong horizon; simplify complex landscape into near/mid/far painted cel background layers.

## Linework
Sparse confident landmark contours; use value boundaries for distant masses instead of outlining every tree.

## Palette
Terrain and weather colors from source; contrast naturally with one warm/cool depth progression.

## Shadow grammar
Hard-edge foreground rock/tree shade, softer distance separation only as actual atmosphere suggests.

## Background policy
Do not replace the location; faithfully regroup foliage/clouds and preserve distinctive natural shapes.

## Preservation guardrails
Mountain outline, coast/shore boundaries, landmark positions, vegetation type and scale relationships.

## Negative constraints
No invented pagodas, celestial phenomena, anime cities or sunrise not in photograph.

## Quality checks
Location remains recognizable and depth is created through drawn layers, not a photo blur.
