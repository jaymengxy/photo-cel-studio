# macro-nature

## When to use
Flowers, plants, insects, feathers, biological textures and small-scale outdoor close-ups.

## Source cues
petals, leaves, wing veins, insect limbs, natural symmetry, selective shallow focus.

## Composition strategy
Protect observed specimen structure; emphasize primary biological silhouette and scale cues.

## Linework
Vary outline weight by form; deliberate interior contour detail for petal, antenna and wing structure.

## Palette
Use original leaf/petal/body colors as clean fill groups; one chromatic accent for focal point.

## Shadow grammar
Small angular form shadows from observed micro-lighting; avoid plastic 3D smoothness.

## Background policy
Reduce distracting macro bokeh to cel background color fields without inventing habitat.

## Preservation guardrails
Petal arrangement, leaf count where discernible, insect legs/wing count and specimen geometry.

## Negative constraints
No imaginary species, misplaced wings, extra stamens/legs or random fantasy mushrooms.

## Quality checks
Biological subject remains structurally correct and clearly recognizable.
