# portrait-character

## When to use
Single or grouped face-focused portraits, selfies and character-forward candid shots.

## Source cues
face occupies significant area, distinctive hair, glasses, wrinkles, expression, hands and clothing.

## Composition strategy
Keep lens angle and eye line; place the real character in a strong balanced animated frame.

## Linework
Mature expressive features with purposeful facial line weight; selective wrinkles and eye shapes rather than generic big eyes.

## Palette
Source-respecting skin tones, hair/clothes identity accents and clear cell-color steps.

## Shadow grammar
Facial planar shadows designed around observed light direction, restrained hard-edge cheek/neck shapes.

## Background policy
Simplify setting while keeping personal/environmental context if central to portrait.

## Preservation guardrails
Per-person differences in face shape, age, hairstyle, glasses, visible accessories, expression and posture.

## Negative constraints
No face swapping, automatic rejuvenation, generic idol/anime face or gender reinterpretation.

## Quality checks
Distinctive person remains recognizable at portrait crop; not a beautification filter.
