# sci-fi-industrial

## When to use
Machines, infrastructure, factories, transport installations or explicitly requested conceptual science-fiction treatment.

## Source cues
pipes, vents, metal stairways, towers, industrial grilles, hard mechanical geometry.

## Composition strategy
Organize observed mechanical planes into clear depth and precise vanishing points; apply futuristic setting redesign only with permission.

## Linework
Technical but economical hard-surface contours; deliberately weighted silhouette against structure.

## Palette
Desaturated industrial grays/teals and source-supported safety/accent colors.

## Shadow grammar
Strong angular shadow plates and controlled metallic reflection shapes.

## Background policy
Keep existing built environment recognizable; drawing simplification must not create impossible machinery.

## Preservation guardrails
Authentic connections, proportions and all identifiable industrial/human elements.

## Negative constraints
No unrequested cyborgs, robots, futuristic advertising or fabricated city landmarks.

## Quality checks
Perspective and engineering logic survive despite artistic simplification.
