# urban-cinematic

## When to use
Candid street, public parks, markets, pathways, urban daily-life documentary scenes whose location and social context are the story.

## Source cues
street furniture, crosswalks, vendors, benches, context-rich pedestrians, layered city depths.

## Composition strategy
Use existing camera height, street perspective and incidental human relationships as a filmed establishing or medium shot; simplify distant clutter into layered background paintings.

## Linework
Moderate strong figure outlines; lighter architectural/background ink so the city does not overpower the gesture.

## Palette
Keep original local color families, muted secondary colors, one readable accent from clothing/signage.

## Shadow grammar
Planar building and cast shadows; characters get a clear base/shadow divide consistent with actual sun.

## Background policy
Simplify texture, keep signs and recognizable location structure without fabricating legible lettering.

When mature-ova is selected, apply `references/mature-cel-render.md`: flat pedestrians with few garment folds, restrained source-derived road/building shadow masses and opaque painted old-city surfaces where source-supported. Gray-blue grouping requires compatible source hues or an explicit cool-restrained palette. At night retain existing localized sign/window colors, reduce repetitive bright details, keep dry roads dry. Real ages, crowd density, gestures and source light locations outrank paint simplification. Other profiles keep their baselines.

## Preservation guardrails
People count, street position, gesture, sidewalk geometry, key urban details, cultural specificity.

## Negative constraints
No default futuristic city, random floating props, cloned passersby, fake signage or new weather.

## Quality checks
The candid event reads at thumbnail size and the city is drawn, not a blurred photograph.
