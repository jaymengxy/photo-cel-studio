# youth-energetic

## When to use
Children playing, laughing, group interaction, joyful daily-life photography where expressive acting dominates.

## Source cues
playful hands, expressive faces, colorful clothes, laughter, small-scale interpersonal energy.

## Composition strategy
Keep the children's relative placement and playful direction; choose vibrant clear silhouette but leave their real interaction intact.

## Linework
Friendly crisp facial/garment contours and strong hand gestures without oversized childlike cartoon eyes.

## Palette
Source-derived bright accent colors with clear flat shadow separations; avoid global candy saturation.

## Shadow grammar
Short hard-edged forms with consistent daylight direction, readable skin tones.

## Background policy
Use legible everyday backgrounds; declutter distracting boxes and distant signage.

## Preservation guardrails
Actual ages, facial features, clothing colors, relative positions and real hand-to-person contact.

## Negative constraints
No chibi, romanticization, beautified adult faces, changed number, oversized eyes or invented game.

## Quality checks
Animation scene feels lively without changing children into another age or action.
