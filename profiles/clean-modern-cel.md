# clean-modern-cel

## Profile ID
`clean-modern-cel` — optional modern contrast, not the default.

## When to use
Explicit clean contemporary cel request, a same-source comparison, or authorized auto routing for a bright uncluttered photo without a stronger candidate. Keep credible adult/animal proportions.

## Visual intent
A precise contemporary hand-drawn cel frame: cleaner stroke control, brighter source-derived color separation and a minimal medium finish. Observable contrast to mature-ova comes from drawing and color decisions, not a modern label in the prompt.

## Linework
Use precise smooth contours with consistent pressure within each stroke family. Outer silhouettes remain heavier than inner facial, fold and component lines; fewer pressure fluctuations and irregular ink joins than mature-ova. Retain structural breaks and expressive acting; do not trace every photographic edge or create thick identical cartoon outlines.

## Color palette
Use clearer, brighter source hue groups and cleaner local contrast than mature-ova while keeping identity colors and skin/coat hues. Secondary planes can be more chromatically distinct, but no fluorescent candy sweep or erased source value relationships. Resolve omitted palette to `clear-bright`, not the global restrained default.

## Shadow grammar
Two or three sharply bounded values per material: smooth flat base, crisp shadow and limited graphic highlight. Design readable face, fabric and vehicle volumes from existing light; do not soften shapes into digital gradients. Shadow borders are more regular than mature-ova, never arbitrary geometric decals.

## Background painting
Use clean drawn environmental shapes, tidy controlled paint edges, clear near/mid/far separation and sparse surface marks. Preserve actual perspective, openings and landmark silhouettes. Do not replace the background with a generic polished travel destination or blur the original photo.

## Character and object treatment
Preserve mature believable anatomy, original age and expression, pet species/markings and vehicle engineering. Cleaner facial line economy does not authorize anime-template face replacement or plastic objects.

## Material / analog finish
Minimal texture: omitted `surface_texture` resolves to `none`. Keep deliberately drawn plates and ink; do not add film grain or aged cel discoloration just because the global preset says subtle-analog. An explicit texture request remains faint, maintaining a visibly cleaner surface than mature-ova.

## Compatible Scene Modes
All registered modes; portrait-character, youth-energetic, food-lifestyle, macro-nature and vehicle-mechanical are useful clean comparisons. Mode does not select or replace this style.

## Atmosphere compatibility
All six when observed. Preserve night contrast, wet reflection shapes and actual rear light without all-over glow. Snow/mist does not justify smoothing all figure shadows.

## Preservation guardrails
P0/P1 identity, count, pose and scene structure remain fixed; preserve original **light direction** and key hues. **Master-lock** retains all approved areas and their prior style. Brightness/intensity controls cannot change the photographed event.

## Negative constraints
No cheap cartoon filter, uniformly thick tracing, glossy 3D/PBR materials, chibi, generic idol faces, invented neon or smooth digital shading. Do not accidentally apply mature-ova's grain, heavy irregular ink or blanket-muted secondary palette.

## Quality checks
Outer/inner hierarchy and 2–3 hard values are visible; strokes are steadier, colors clearer/brighter and surface cleaner than mature-ova using the same source. Subjects remain mature and specific. No gloss, photo texture or low-cost cartoon-overlay appearance.
