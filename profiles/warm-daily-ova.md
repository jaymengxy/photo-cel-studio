# warm-daily-ova

## Profile ID
`warm-daily-ova` — restrained domestic cel treatment; never automatic cuteness.

## When to use
Explicit warm everyday style or authorized auto for pet/family/domestic interaction with a lived-in setting. Pets under unspecified style still default to mature-ova.

## Visual intent
Natural, approachable daily-life acting, gently warm secondary paint groups, stable cel form shadows and tactile hand-painted surroundings. Warmth comes from palette/line decisions, not invented expressions, a sunset or juvenile proportions.

## Linework
Readable, softly tapered outer strokes with lighter eye, mouth, hand and fabric structure; sparse fur clusters follow the actual coat silhouette. Keep a controlled hand-ink irregularity. Rounded lines remain anatomically specific, not uniformly bubbly cartoon outlines.

## Color palette
Warm restrained cream/ochre/earth secondary groups where compatible with the source, with original skin, fur markings, clothes and room material hues intact. Preserve cool daylight, blue cast surfaces or cold snow when observed. Do not make every wall orange or inject golden-hour light into an interior.

## Shadow grammar
Use 2–3 stable flat values with hard-edged rounded form masses around cheeks, fur, garment folds and furniture. Softer mood does not mean soft gradient shadows; contact shapes and illuminated planes follow the original light. Small warm highlights require an actual warm source.

## Background painting
Redraw existing furnishings, windows, food/table objects and room planes as warm tactile paint masses with lighter distant lines. Retain furniture layout, opening positions and domestic wear when visible; simplify incidental texture without inventing décor or tidying away meaningful props.

## Character and object treatment
Natural expressions and original gaze/gesture, real ages and proportions, breed-specific eyes/ears/coat and authentic object interaction. Friendly does not mean smiling by default; preserve a sleeping pet, stern face or quiet elder as photographed.

## Material / analog finish
Subtle paint/cel variation and fine medium texture, with clear surfaces and readable facial/fur detail. No watercolor wash over subjects, paper grain filter, sugary bloom or plush/plastic toy finish.

## Compatible Scene Modes
Pet-character, portrait-character, interior-atmosphere, food-lifestyle, quiet-dramatic, everyday-still-life and youth-energetic are strong pairings; all registered modes can retain their object/geometry rules.

## Atmosphere compatibility
All six when source-supported. Cold rain/snow/night remains cold where observed; warm lamps stay localized. Golden-hour or backlit is not added by this profile alone. Keep physical compatibility checks from the atmosphere registry.

## Preservation guardrails
P0/P1 identity, expression, count, anatomy, props and camera stay intact. Keep original **light direction**, time/weather and coat/clothing colors. **Master-lock** freezes accepted expressions, background and finish outside the specified edit.

## Negative constraints
No kawaii/chibi, oversized eyes, infantilization, animal clothes, humanoid paws, invented smiles or added family members. No new warm lamps/sunlight, decorative glow, fake cozy furnishings or digital airbrushed shading.

## Quality checks
Warm secondary colors and tapered lines differ from mature-ova without altering source light. Hard cel values visibly shape faces/fur/furniture; surroundings look hand-painted and lived-in. Expressions, ages and pet identity remain recognizable; warmth never becomes a cute filter.
